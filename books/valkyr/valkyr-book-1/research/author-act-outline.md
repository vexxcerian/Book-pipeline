# VALKYR — Book One: the author's act outline

Transcribed from Section 8 of the author's series bible (`research/valkyr-source.pdf`),
2026-09-07. **This is the binding roadmap.** The architect may re-shape HOW these beats are
distributed across chapters — chapter count, chapter length, section labels, order within an
act — but may not drop a beat, add a new act-level turn, or change where the book ends.

The chapter numbers below ("Ch. 1–8ish") are the author's rough thinking, not a specification.
Section 0.5 of `book-architect.md` decides the actual macro-structure.

---

## Act One — Recruitment (Ch. 1–8ish)

- Open on Vexx's retirement and the four-year-old grief of Rx's "death" (a condensed,
  dramatized version of the origins piece already drafted).
- **The approach:** Vexx learns Rx survived as an AI and will bond with no one else. He
  re-enters Valkyr.
- Introduce **Jameson as Vexx's trusted commanding officer** — warm, credible, a man Vexx has
  reason to respect. **Plant small wrongness here in hindsight-only details:** a look, a
  too-practiced condolence, something Rx flinches at that neither of them can explain.
- Vexx is tasked with **rebuilding Valkyr from the ground up.** He assembles the founding
  roster: Goliath/Spector, Gaia/Echo, Paladin/Bastion, Zeus/Hollow, Aglaope/Requiem.
- **End Act One** on the team's first mission briefing together — stepping into the field as a
  unit for the first time.

## Act Two — First Cracks (Ch. 9–20ish)

- Structure as **2–3 mission arcs that double as character studies** for the roster:
  - Goliath/Spector's Phantom reveal.
  - Paladin's first friction with a Choosing-adjacent order.
  - Gaia's friction with Vexx's command style.
- Thread the **first and second memory-unlock stages** through these missions — a flash Rx
  can't explain during one op, a glitch of his own voice during another, both dismissed or
  half-noticed.
- Vexx starts an **informal, mostly private investigation** — pulling old mission logs, noticing
  inconsistencies in how Rx's death was documented, a name in a witness log that doesn't match
  the official account.
- **Aglaope/Requiem emerges as Vexx's off-record confidante** — a scene where he almost tells
  her what he's starting to suspect.
- **End Act Two** on a mission where **Jameson is present in person** for the first time since
  Vexx's recruitment — and Rx has a **severe reaction (stage-three leak: proximity-triggered)**,
  frightening enough that Vexx can no longer explain it away.

## Act Three — Closing In (Ch. 21–30ish)

- Vexx's investigation and Rx's fracturing memory now **openly feed each other** — Vexx pushes,
  Rx flinches harder, until Vexx confronts him directly.
- **Rx cannot give Vexx the full truth yet** (full unlock is reserved for Book Two) but gives him
  enough — a name, a location, a fragment of the refusal itself — to make clear **this wasn't
  combat. It was murder.** And the shape of the motive (something bigger than a cover-up — a man
  building power) starts to become visible.
- **Vexx realizes the man he suspects is the same man he currently reports to.**
- **End Book One** on Vexx **choosing to keep investigating in secret rather than act** — the
  team now aware something is wrong, Jameson still unaware he's been made, and the reader left
  with the same dread Vexx is carrying: he's about to spend Book Two hunting the man who
  commands him.

## Threads deliberately left open for Book Two

- Full memory unlock and the complete truth of the founding incident.
- Jameson realizing he's been discovered.
- Whether Paladin's moral objections escalate into open conflict with command.
- The Jameson confrontation and fall.
- First hint (can be planted late in Book One, or saved entirely) of the shared AI donor-stock
  twist — a SPARTAN-II AI who might be "kin" to a Valkyr AI.
