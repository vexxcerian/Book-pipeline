### Three seams underneath it

**(1) SIMILE STARVATION — new, and the strongest pipeline finding in this evaluation.**

Three pipeline chapters, all below the author's minimum, monotonically declining, worst in the longest chapter. Ch.8 contains **two** similes in 5,655 words (*"a smell like scorched dust"*, *"a toecap up like glass"*), both physical, both good. Ch.1 contains roughly eighteen.

`style_check.py` sets `simile_per1k` as a **ceiling of 5.0** with **no floor**. That is precisely the omission `docs/QUALITY-GATES.md` documents in writing: *"Told 'no more than 9.5 em-dashes per 1,000 words', a writer scores a safe 4.7 and produces prose that is calm where the author is nervous."* The em-dash floor was added for exactly this reason. The simile floor was not. `voice-dna.md` §4's ≤3.5/1k ceiling and its instruction that "the voice's power comes from restraint" are being read as licence to go to zero — but restraint *below the author's own floor* is not the author's restraint, it is a different writer.

**No Ch.8 edit.** A chapter of flat procedural witness is the correct place for simile scarcity and I would not add one. This is a pipeline fix (F3).

## §THE CONVENTION — the two-register dialogue system, verified, DO NOT "FIX"

A previous evaluator on this book read the author's two-register system as an inconsistency and recommended repairing it, which would have destroyed it. Ch.8 gives that trap a new surface, so I am closing it in writing.

**Ch.8's practice, audited line by line:**

| Form | Used in Ch.8 for | Correct? |
|---|---|---|
| Unquoted italics | The flag summary (a document, twice, character-identical) | ✓ |
| Unquoted italics | Rx's private channel — 4 turns, one scene | ✓ |
| Unquoted italics | Echo *"on the net, through Gaia's interface"* while Gaia is 400 m forward and alone | ✓ |
| Unquoted italics | *routine welfare and readiness* · *and again, and this time listen to the second one* · the word *sorry* · the word *relieved* — all speech named inside **summary** narration | ✓ |
| “Quotation marks” | All dramatized-scene speech, **including Bastion and Spector speaking through interfaces into a room** | ✓ |
| ‘Single quotes’ | *"‘Unstable.’ And ‘refused.’"* — words cited inside quoted speech | ✓ |

**The apparent contradiction — Echo in italics, Spector in quotes, both AIs, both in scene — is not a contradiction, and the author settles it himself in his own drafted Ch.2:** *"'Confirmed,' Spector said, through Goliath's interface, clipped and certain."* Quoted, in the author's hand, AI speech, dramatized scene.

**The operative test, stated so no future pass has to re-derive it: is the voice arriving over the channel, or is it in the room?** Rx and Echo are on the channel; Bastion and Spector are voiced into a shared physical space. Ch.8 applies the rule correctly in every instance. **No change. Nothing here is a defect.**
