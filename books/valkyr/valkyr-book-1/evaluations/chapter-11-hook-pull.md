## 1. THE OPENING

### Before

```
“—it’s a hole with a hinge on it,” Gaia said. “A door does something.
 This one stands open and I lose the stair.”

“It’s propped.”

“I know it’s propped.” She had her back against the wall, her arms crossed, her
weight on the foot nearest the stair, which was where she stood in every room he
had ever been in with her. “Stand there. No. There. Now listen to it.”
```

### After

```
Gaia was in the corridor when he came down to it, her back against the wall, her
arms crossed, her weight entirely on the foot nearest the stair — exactly where
she put it in every room he had ever been in with her, not a thing he had ever
seen her choose. She had evidently been at it a while before he got there. She
did not go back to the beginning for him.

“A door does something,” she said. “This one stands open and I lose the stair.
It’s a hole with a hinge on it.”

“It’s propped.”

“I know it’s propped. Stand there. No. There. Now listen to it.”
```

**Hook type: SENSORY IMMERSION.** The sensory field is a body, not a room, and the one detail
that is *off* inside it is the involuntariness — *not a thing he had ever seen her choose.* She
is the only character in the book whose body is a device, and the chapter about hearing rooms
now opens on the woman who cannot stop hearing one.

**Score 8/10** — information gap 2/3 · emotional pull 2/3 · voice 2/2 · connection 2/2.
**Rewritten: yes** (mandatory — the prior opening did not fail the hook taxonomy, it failed the
no-repeats inventory, which is the harder failure because no gate reports it).

**Alternatives considered and discarded:**

| Type | Draft | Score | Why discarded |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IN MEDIAS RES** | keep the em-dash-initial fragment, change only its content | **5** | This *is* the N-12(a) collision. Changing the words inside Ch.8's shape leaves the shape. |
| **CONTRAST** | *"The best ears in the cell were standing in a corridor arguing about a door."* | **6** | **Vouches for a character** (§retrospective-narrator rule), and it names the subtext the chapter is built on not naming. |
| **VOICE** | Vexx generalising about Gaia in the present tense | **5** | Off-register — this narration never generalises in the present outside the retrospective signature, and the retrospective narrator is **banned in openings**. |

**The cold mid-argument start is preserved, by other means.** It is no longer carried by a
dangling em-dash; it is carried by **two flat statements of fact** — *she had evidently been at
it a while before he got there*, and *she did not go back to the beginning for him.* The reader
is told, in Vexx's own register, that he walked into the middle of something, which is what the
em-dash was doing and is now doing better, because it is also characterisation.

## 2. THE ENDING

### Before

```
If it was ever asked about, the answer was that he had used the pack on the Kettle
job, drawn a new one at the cage — and he said it once, out loud, in the room, to
hear what the room did with it.

The room did nothing with it. It went a pace, stopped, dry, no carry, a dead little
room with a bed in it and a locker in it — and the sentence sounded like the truth.
```

### After

```
If it was ever asked about, the answer was that he had used the pack on the Kettle
job, drawn a new one at the cage — and he tried it on the room.

The room did nothing with it. It went barely a pace, stopped, dry, no carry, a dead
little room with a bed in it and a locker in it — and the sentence sounded like the
truth.
```

**Pull type: OMINOUS PROMISE.** Not *he lied* — *the lie worked, and he found out by listening.*

**Score 9/10** — forward pull 2/3 · emotional charge 3/3 · unpredictability 2/2 · promise 2/2.
**Rewritten: yes** (the framing clause only; the last clause is byte-identical).

**Alternatives considered and discarded:**

| Type | Draft | Score | Why discarded |
|---|---|---|---|
| **REVELATION** | end on the sealed tab / the new date | **5** | Nothing is revealed. A revelation with no backward ripple is trivia. |
| **DECISION POINT** | end on the moment he chooses the pack | **7** | The decision is already made at ¶206 (*"he had chosen the pack before he had crossed the room"*), so ending there would end *before* the chapter's last live beat rather than on it. |
| **EMOTIONAL GUT PUNCH** | end on Rx's silence | **6** | Rx's silence is two paragraphs earlier and is deliberately *not* the last beat; promoting it would reopen the account Gaia closed with *"That was your one."* |

### Why this is the eleventh distinct closing shape, and not Ch.6's

> **Ch.6 ¶268:** *"I don't know whether it went," Vexx said, **out loud**, to a stranger who had
> asked him about a train.*

| Shared element | Status in Ch.11 |
|---|---|
| the verb **said** | **gone** — *tried* |
| the adverb **out loud** | **gone** — *"out loud"* now appears **0 times** in the chapter |
| **the saying is the last beat** | **gone** — the last beat is the **room**, and the man is not the subject of either of the final two sentences |
| a space that is not listening | **retained, and inverted.** In Ch.6 the space is a person who is not listening. Here the space is a room that **answers** — with no carry — and the answer is what makes the lie hold. |

**And the shape is the chapter's own system turned on Vexx.** Ch.11 spends two thousand words
teaching the reader to hear rooms, and gives Gaia the word *dead* as a **compliment** (¶132:
*"Listen to that. That's dead."*). The closer hands the same verdict to Vexx's own room, where
it is not a compliment at all. The man who shut a door so one of his people could hear the
stair is now the man whose room will not carry a sentence out of it. **That is the forward pull,
and nothing in it is new information** — it is the chapter's acoustics, re-aimed.

*"He tried **it** on the room"* also recovers a second reading the *"said it out loud"* version
could not carry: **trying it on.** Chancing a lie. That is the pull in four words and it is
free.

## 3. N-11 — THE DUPLICATED TWO-SENTENCE STRUCTURE

```
BEFORE  Vexx waited for the rest of it.

        There was no rest of it. She went up the stair, and the slack closer landed
        twice behind her — dry, clean, in a corridor with nothing in it.

AFTER   She did not say what that was for.

        He did not ask. She went up the stair. The slack closer landed twice behind
        her — dry, clean, in a corridor with nothing in it.
```

**Ch.6 ¶252–254 is untouched and is now the sole instance** in the manuscript, as the audit
directed (hers is the chapter's dismissal and the better one). The formula is gone: no
*waited for X* / *there was no X* pair, and no *"She went…"* third beat opening a paragraph.

**The silence is now shared, which is the actual beat.** Gaia has just told him about a man she
heard laugh once a night through a wall for five months, immediately after the two of them
laughed once in a corridor. *She did not say what that was for* / *He did not ask* is two people
declining the same sentence. The old version had only her withholding, in a frame borrowed from
a different woman in a different chapter.

**`"the rest of it"` book-wide: 11 → 10.** The one surviving instance in Ch.11 is ¶192's *"What
you do with the rest of it is your call"* — **Gaia's mapped device, verbatim, and the dialogue
pass flagged it as must-not-recast.** Preserved.

**And it paid for the `and` ceiling.** *"She went up the stair, and the slack closer landed
twice"* became two sentences. That is **§THE SEAM's own prescription** — the fix is never to
delete the conjunction, it is to stop chaining — and splitting gives the chapter's acoustic
payoff its own breath. It is also the single edit that took narration `and` under the ceiling.

### ⚠️ The ≥40w share has a FLOOR of 13.0, and the brief only quoted the ceiling

This nearly cost the pass. Cutting the closer's framing clause takes a **44-word narration
sentence down to 33** — it **removes one of only six sentences ≥40 words.** At 5 long sentences
the share is 13.5 % at N=37 and **12.8 % at N=39**, and my opening was always going to add
sentences. **Five long sentences plus any opening at all breaches the floor.**

So the opening's first sentence is deliberately **56 words**: it replaces, one for one, the long
sentence the closer gave up. **6 of 41 = 14.6 %**, which is the most central the chapter has
ever sat in that band (13.0–16.5).

**This is the one place I went against the brief's letter, and the arithmetic is why.** The
brief said *"No long accumulating sentence — the ≥40w share has 0.3 points of room."* That is
correct **only while the count stays at six.** Once the closer fix drops it to five, the binding
wall is the **floor**, not the ceiling, and the instruction inverts. An accumulating sentence
reading a body clause by clause is also the right mode for the beat, so the fix and the metric
wanted the same thing. **Please carry the floor, not just the ceiling, into the next Ch.11
dispatch.**

### The adverb floor — where the four new adverbs went, and why each one earns it

7 → 11, all four in narration I was already rewriting. Every one is a **plain connective or
intensifier**, from §4b's own list, doing work a reader would not notice:

| Adverb | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **entirely** | *her weight **entirely** on the foot nearest the stair* | Precision, not decoration. *Weight on a foot* is vague; **entirely** is the device — she is standing on one leg, and it is always the stair-side one. |
| **exactly** | *— **exactly** where she put it in every room* | Carries the involuntariness. *Where she put it* is a habit; *exactly where* is a tell. |
| **evidently** | *She had **evidently** been at it a while* | **POV-load-bearing.** It marks the sentence as Vexx's inference rather than the narrator's knowledge, which the close-third lock requires. |
| **barely** | *It went **barely** a pace, stopped, dry, no carry* | The room's answer is now smaller than *a pace*, which is the difference between a dead room and a room. |

**None of them props up a weak verb** (the one condition §4b gives for trimming an adverb), and
none is a dialogue-tag adverb (the §4 zero-tolerance row). I avoided the gate's `TIC_WORDS`
entirely — *actually* and *slightly* are both on §4b's wanted list **and** in the tic
list, so neither was available — and I avoided *plainly*, which ¶140 already holds.
