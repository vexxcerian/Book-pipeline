## Anti-AI Scan — reported only where it hurts the page

- **F1 — "the particular" x5.** *the particular way a team goes quiet* · *the particular
  stillness* · *the particular quiet focus* · *the particular clarity that comes from proximity
  to death* · *the particular unease of a man witnessing a skill*. Two of them fall within
  sixteen lines. The construction is the author's own and belongs in the book; at this density
  the narrator sounds like he has one adjective, and the reader starts hearing the frame rather
  than the noun. Ch.1 uses it three times in 3,911 words and every use lands.
- **The "filed it away / not yet a problem" beat fires twice inside one chapter**, and the
  second firing is a near-verbatim reprint of Ch.2's stack: *"He filed it away. Not yet a
  problem. A shape on the horizon, closer now than it had been that morning, though he still
  didn't have the vocabulary to name it."* A third variant appears at line 113 (*"He simply
  filed it, the way he'd been filing everything that autumn"*). See F3 and the summary report.
- **#11 Explanatory extension — F4.** Zeus's interrogation is dramatized and then explained:
  *"…until Vexx realized, watching the operator's shoulders slowly uncoil over the better part
  of twenty minutes, that Zeus was building trust before he built a timeline — letting the man
  talk about his squad, their names, small human details that had nothing to do with the
  ambush, before circling, gently, back toward the thing that mattered."* The scene already
  showed this. The sentence takes the discovery away from the reader.
- **Clear and working:** em-dash 9.1/1k (lowest in the five), similes, long sentences.

## Reader Reports
**Devourer:** No stopping point. *"Valkyr. Took you long enough"* is the best mid-chapter turn
in the five and the reader will not put the book down after it.
**Critic:** Underlines the comms array, the dropped-weight line, and Corwin's laugh. Winces at
the fifth "the particular." Notes that the Zeus interrogation is the chapter's finest
sequence and that the paragraph explaining it is the chapter's worst.
**Hostile:** Two challenges, both answerable. (1) *How does Corwin know the word "Valkyr"?* —
the chapter dramatizes the whole cell registering that this is impossible, which is the correct
handling; it is a promise, not a hole. (2) *Vexx omits physical evidence from a report to his
own CO on his first deployment.* The narration flags the instinct as unexamined, which is
exactly right, but the reader may want one concrete beat of him **hesitating over the file**
rather than the omission being reported after the fact.
**Casual Reader:** 8.5/10. The jungle is tactile, the found man is a great scene, and the
"I saw a treeline" moment is the first time the book scares them.

## Strengths to PRESERVE
- **Corwin, entire.** *"So which is it. You here to bring me in, or you here to finish what my
  own chain of command already tried to start?"* — question marks deliberately absent, which is
  the author's signature punctuation and should never be normalized by a grammar pass.
- **The Rx treeline exchange.** Fragments, restarts, the too-quick *"I'm fine"* sharpened into
  urgency. This is the voice breaking correctly and it is the book's central mechanism working.
- **The smashed comms array.** Do not touch.
- **The deliberately incomplete report**, and the refusal to examine it.

  8.5 on that surgery, with F4 and F5 as reinforcement. No new writing is required.
