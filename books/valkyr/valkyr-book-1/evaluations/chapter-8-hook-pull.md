## What was changed, and why

**Scene 3 → 4 (the join into the failed stack). Rewritten.**

Before: *"Outside, Aglaope was standing about six metres off the plant house door with her back
to the wind, not looking in — she stayed there for the entire time they were in the building,
did not come in, did not go anywhere else."*

After: *"Outside, Aglaope had her back to the wind about six metres off the plant house door,
not looking in, not coming in, not going anywhere else — she was still standing in the same
place, at the same distance, when the last of them came out."*

Three faults, one fix:
1. **It decelerated into the fast middle.** The original closed on three trailing negatives in
   descending order of interest, ending on the throwaway one (*did not go anywhere else*). The
   sentence decayed at exactly the join the outline wants taut. The negatives are now a rising
   tricolon in the middle of the sentence and the weight has moved to the close.
2. **It rhymed with the next scene's closer.** Scene 4 ends on a negation (*…had not been
   reaching for the thermos*). Two consecutive scene-ends closing on *did not / had not*, both
   long single sentences, both about Aglaope perceiving something nobody else did, is the same
   pull wearing different clothes. The rewrite ends positive and temporal, so the two Aglaope
   plants now escalate instead of repeat.
3. **POV.** *"she stayed there for the entire time they were in the building"* is retrospective
   knowledge Vexx cannot have while inside. Anchoring it to *when the last of them came out*
   puts the fact back inside his perception. Content, beat, and the fact that nobody posted her
   are unchanged.

Length was held deliberately: 42w → 45w, still one sentence in the ≥40w bucket, one em-dash,
no new *and*.

**Scene 4 (three sentences from its end). Rewritten — sequencing, not quality.**

Before: *"Nobody else said anything."* After: *"Nobody else came up on the net."*

The original line was fine on its own. It was changed because scene 5's closing region contains
*"Nobody in that room said anything."* — the identical construction, doing the identical job
(silence as the answer), in the closing three sentences of two consecutive scenes. Scene 5's is
untouchable: the silence between Merrick's two utterances *is* the anchor beat. So scene 4's
moved. The new line is also more accurate to the exchange, which happened on the net, and
*"and that was the only comment anyone made"* still carries the room-level silence.

## Considered and declined

- **Scenes 5 and 6 both open "They gave… / They took it…".** A real syntactic rhyme, but the
  collective *They* is the chapter's procedural register and is doing the outline's stated work
  (*the chapter that makes the cell a unit rather than a roster*). Every rewrite tested made it
  worse: leading on the clock (*At eleven at night…*) puts two consecutive scenes on a
  timestamp, since scene 7 opens *"The transport came at half past six the next morning."*
- **Scenes 1 and 2 both end on Gaia speaking.** Two, not three, and the shapes differ
  completely — scene 1 is narration-final at 24 words and closes on Vexx's realisation; scene 2
  is a 3-word dialogue snap. Left.
- **Moving scene 2's shed line (*"a man who skips the shed…"*) to final position.** It is a
  stronger pull than the Kettle punchline, but it would have made scenes 2, 3 and 4 three
  consecutive long-single-sentence closers — a worse violation than the one it fixed. It also
  costs the ≥40w floor if split. Left.
- **Scenes 5 and 6 both end on short quoted speech.** Two, not three; different types (gut
  punch vs decision point), different charge, and scene 6's snap cannot be softened without
  changing dialogue. Left.
- **Scenes 8 and 9 both open on a static thing in a place plus a neglect clause** (*where
  Goliath had left it* / *Nobody had put in for it*). Judged a designed echo — Paladin's
  corridor, then Vexx's — not drift. Left.
- **Scene 4's four-paragraph decompression** (*That was mine* → *Copy* → the thermos → the
  flash-forward). Reads as a gentle landing on the page but is the gear change: it is the only
  thing decelerating the fast middle into the very slow assessment. Left.

## The five-gear reading-speed design

| Join | Outline gear | Now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 → 2 | slow → slow | Humanity to hostile ground. No acceleration wanted, none applied. |
| 2 → 3 | slow → tightening | Punchline hard-stop into *"They found him in the plant house."* The tonal drop is the point: laugh, then Merrick. |
| **3 → 4** | **tightening → FAST** | **Fixed.** The closer now compresses to a seal instead of decaying, and hands into *"The one thing that went wrong that day went wrong at the relay hall, at fourteen minutes past three. It was Vexx's."* — front-loaded, timestamped, four words of blame. This was the defect. |
| 4 → 5 | FAST → VERY SLOW | The *"Later — much later"* flash-forward is the brake, and scene 5 opens on a clause-chained 40-word sentence. Correct. Nothing hurries the assessment. |
| 5 → 6 | very slow → slow | Dead stop on *"Before dark"*, cold procedural pickup. Correct. |
| 6 → 7 | slow → FAST | Snap on *"Then put it on."* into *"The transport came at half past six the next morning."* Fastest join in the chapter. |
| 7 → 8 | FAST → DEAD SLOW | Ramp up, transport gone, then a bag against a wall. Correct. |
| 8 → 9 | dead slow → dead slow | *"There was nothing in it that time either."* into the blinking light nobody reported. Flat to flat, by design. |

**No shareable moment was manufactured.** Both rewritten sentences were deliberately kept
plain and procedural. The scene 3 closer was tested against a sharper variant ending on *"did
not once look in"* and rejected: it was quotable, and it re-created the negation rhyme.

**POV.** All nine endings sit inside Vexx's perception. Scene 4's flash-forward is Aglaope
speaking within his hearing on the transport; the scene 3 rewrite moves a retrospective summary
back inside his direct observation.
