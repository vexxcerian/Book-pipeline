## The closing shape — eighth distinct

**Chosen shape: the narrator re-reads a document *while adding the fourth instance of it
himself*.** Vexx writes the report exactly as he promised — including Paladin's objection, in
Paladin's words, in writing — then scrolls back to the authorisation box and reads the flag
summary once before sending. The sentence is reproduced unchanged, and stops.

Why it is not Ch.7's shape: Ch.7 hands the reader a raw found block and walks away from it.
Here the document is one already-known sentence, quoted inside a scene, read by a character
performing an action on the same form — and what re-shades it is the beat immediately before,
the act of putting a subordinate's third objection *in writing*. The move is authorship, not
discovery. No gloss, no comment, no reaction line after it.

Not dramatic irony (Ch.2), not a scene beat with another character (Ch.3 — Aglaope exits
three paragraphs early on purpose), not an interior equation (Ch.4), not an object in the dark
(Ch.5), not a misdirected answer to a stranger (Ch.6). Inside Vexx's perception throughout.

## Scene 8 — the corridor

- **POV solution.** Vexx goes back to the relay hall for the breach bag at a little after
  seven. He hears it before he is through the near door, stops inside the frame, and does not
  pick the bag up — the bag a metre from his hand is the whole cost of witnessing without
  entering. He leaves and re-walks the fuel shed he already cleared, both walls, the full
  twenty-two minutes, and there is nothing in it that time either. That is the price, unnamed.
- **No editorialising.** Paladin's break is three physical facts (head down, the breathing
  going and coming back, the heel of the hand against the mouth) and one flat verdict on the
  gesture — "which did not help". No cause given anywhere; the chapter already gave it.
- **Goliath's procedural kindness.** He arrives with the spanner from the fuel shed — the
  spanner Spector complained about — and rehangs the door that was chained back, i.e. he
  repairs the literal cause of the failed stack, without anyone naming it. "Take the weight."
  / "Other side." — that second line is Bastion's morning instruction about the right hand
  paying off with Bastion nowhere in the scene. Then "Good." Neither man says anything about
  anything. "Neither of them looked at the near door" is the only acknowledgement in the scene
  and it is about a door.
- **Displacement chaos (Vexx):** he reads the maker's stamp on the last link of the chain
  while a man he commands is coming apart eight feet away.

## Scene 9 — the close

- **Rx runs hot,** reported secondhand by Goliath, plainly, once, with no theory attached:
  "Spector wants something said to you. He wants it said once." Three words; Goliath has two —
  **"unstable" and "refused."** Goliath has no interest in what they mean and does not
  speculate. No Rx interiority; **Rx does not appear at all in the last 400 words of the
  chapter**, and Vexx never asks him anything. That silence is deliberate and is the sharpest
  unanswered question the chapter loads.
- **The deflection.** Vexx converts a statement about his brother into a maintenance ticket
  ("Interface load is logged on every operation… it goes up to the technicians with the rest
  of the telemetry"). Goliath does not accept it — "That isn't what he did. He came to me" —
  then asks his one question, "Do you want the third one, when he gives it to me?", and waits
  past the point where a man expects an answer. Vexx answers by drinking the coffee and saying
  "It's the water." **Goliath lets him, on the page, by picking the subject up and carrying
  it**: "It is the water. It's been the water since August." Not fooled; does not press. Both
  facts are shown, neither stated.
- **Chaos (Vexx), unprompted memory, badly timed:** the tin of fudge with a lighthouse painted
  on the lid, terrible, gone by the afternoon — landing one line after the word "refused."
- **Secondary chaos (Aglaope):** she comes in without knocking, squares the notebook to the
  edge of the desk with two fingers, and volunteers the one detail that is about *her* —
  "I looked there twice."
- **The notebook decision: it did NOT go out with him.** It surfaces behind the fuse box on
  the shelf brackets after the transport has lifted. Nobody comments; nobody proposes sending
  it on; Vexx does not touch it. It simply sits on the desk beside the pad for the length of
  the report.

## Impulse deviations from the brief — MARKED FOR PROTECTION

1. **The rehung door.** The brief asked for "the thing Goliath would actually do." What the
   text wanted was for the kindness to be *the repair of the specific failure that nearly got
   a nineteen-year-old shot* — the chained-back door was Vexx's error compounding, and Goliath
   fixes it silently with a man who is crying holding the other end. Kept: it makes the
   kindness procedural, wordless, and about the cell rather than about Paladin.
2. **"Other side."** Not in the brief. Bastion's earlier hand-check pays off through Goliath's
   mouth without Bastion present, and Paladin obeys without a word about it.
3. **Vexx re-walking the fuel shed.** The chapter's boring procedure that paid nothing, done a
   second time, immediately after the corridor. Not planned; it is the cost of not entering,
   and it says it without a syllable of interiority.
4. **The last four words of the report beat** — "which he did not shorten." Unplanned. It is
   what makes the flag sentence re-read as being about Paladin.
