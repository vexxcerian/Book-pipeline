# sample-11 — THE SIX AI VOICES (cover-the-name calibration)
**Type:** dialogue / differentiation · **Source:** calibrated for the manuscript. Not the author's prose.

---

Spector: *Charge is on. Three. Two. Set.*

Echo: *—which is going to bring that whole north face down into the cut, and the cut is Marlow, we agreed it was Marlow, and I'd like it on record that I said it first.*

Bastion: *Before anyone moves. Paladin, that knee. Yes or no.*

Hollow: *You asked me this morning whether the armour plating was standard issue. It was reissued.*

Requiem: *…*
Requiem: *Yes. All right. Yes.*

Rx: *Fine. It's the relay. Go.*

---

**Why this sample exists.** **Six AIs sharing a service-cadence register is this book's largest convergence risk.** Six lines, unattributed, and each is identifiable from one axis:

| Line | Axis | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| the countdown nobody needed | **counting — the device belongs to Spector alone** | Spector |
| entering mid-sentence; naming a place and using the name as agreed | never lets a silence stand | Echo |
| body-check before content | asks about bodies, never numbers | Bastion |
| answering a question from this morning, now | withholds until useful | Hollow |
| the pause, then the agreement | the pause *is* the technique | Requiem |
| the too-quick denial | speed — arrives before an honest answer could | Rx |

**The three collisions to police:**
1. **Spector vs Bastion.** Both carry a service cadence. **Bastion counts nothing** — his body-check is a question habit and must never become numeric. If Bastion starts a headcount, that is device bleed.
2. **Echo vs Requiem.** Both are warm. They are exact opposites of technique: Echo has never permitted a silence; Requiem's whole method is the pause.
3. **Hollow vs Voss.** Both withhold. Hollow withholds because it is tactically better later; Voss withholds because explaining is effort she no longer has.

**And the standing prohibition:** no AI in this series uses rampancy vocabulary in any register — no timers, no degradation rates, no "how long do I have." It is the single easiest unearned tension to import from Halo canon, and it is banned.
