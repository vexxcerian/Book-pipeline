# sample-10 — THE DOCUMENT INTERRUPTION (device, at REVEAL)
**Type:** device · **Source:** calibrated for the manuscript (Ch.7 and Ch.14). Not the author's prose. Author precedent: Ch.1's *"Subject can no longer distinguish combat from memory."*

---

*(Ch.7 — the requisition record, quoted cold, no frame:)*

> ISSUE — INDIVIDUAL EQUIPMENT, STANDARD SET
> RECIPIENT: HOLST, D. — SVC NO. [redacted]
> ISSUING OFFICE: 4TH LOGISTICS, SUB-OFFICE 11 / RECLAMATION
> CONDITION AT ISSUE: SERVICEABLE
> RETURN EXPECTED: N/A

Rx read the date out loud. Then he read it again, which he had never once needed to do with a date.

*(Ch.14 — the morning traffic digest, quoted entire:)*

> 2. SUPPLY — Ration variance, Blocks A through D. Quartermaster advises no action.
> 3. CASUALTY — Operator IVES. Hostile contact. Remains recovered. Next of kin notified.
> 4. FACILITIES — Hot water, Block C, restored.

---

**The device, and its three quality levels.**

- **ILLUSTRATE (≤7.0):** the document repeats what the narration just said in colder words. Decorative. **Cut it.**
- **COMMENT (7.5–8.0):** ironic counterpoint. The author's Ch.4 commendation — *"Cell showed excellent judgment and restraint. Well done."* — is already at this level.
- **REVEAL (≥8.5):** the document carries a fact the surrounding prose does not have, **and the reader sees it before Vexx does.** Both examples above are REVEAL. **Target REVEAL or cut the device.**

**Rules it demonstrates:**
- **RECLAMATION appears unglossed in Ch.7 and nobody in the scene notices it.** Eight chapters before it means anything. **Do not let a character or the narration flag it.** It is the book's primary re-read reward and explaining it kills it.
- **The digest's power is entirely positional.** Item 3 is a murder. Item 4 is the hot water. The narration adds nothing.
- **Frequency:** roughly one per two chapters, unevenly distributed, and **absent entirely from Ch.20, Ch.23 and Ch.26**, which belong wholly to people.
- Note the retired construction `never once` in the Ch.7 line — **it is RETIRED at series level and must not survive into the manuscript.** Rewrite it. It is left here as a wear-check exercise.
