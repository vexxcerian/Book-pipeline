# sample-09 — IRRELEVANT THOUGHT (a team, mid-approach)
**Type:** irrelevant thought · **Source:** calibrated for the manuscript (Ch.8/Ch.17 register). Not the author's prose.

---

Two hours out, moving line astern through ground that gave under the boot and gave again, and the conversation on the channel was about coffee.

"It's not the grind," Goliath said. "Nobody in that room has ever had an opinion about the grind. It's the water. You cannot put water at that temperature onto anything and expect it to forgive you."

"You wrote it on the wall," Paladin said.

"I wrote a temperature on the wall. That's not the same as writing on the wall."

Gaia, forty metres ahead and not part of the conversation, said, "This whole valley's dead. You could drop a magazine in here and nobody'd hear it for a hundred metres." Nobody answered her. She said it anyway, the way she said it about corridors and mess halls and the inside of transports, and went back to work.

Vexx let all of it run. Twenty minutes later they found the first of the apology notes Merrick had been leaving for people who were not there, and nobody said anything about coffee again for two days.

---

**Why this sample exists.** A mission chapter is not ninety minutes of tactical excellence. **The competence cascade is this book's single largest AI-default risk** — six elite operators and six AIs, every scene wanting to become people being good at their jobs in sequence.

**Rules it demonstrates:**
- **Two irrelevant obsessions running simultaneously and neither is justified.** Goliath's coffee system; Gaia's acoustics. Neither pays off. Neither becomes a metaphor.
- **Gaia's acoustics line is spoken to nobody and answered by nobody**, and the narration flags that this is normal. That is characterisation by tolerance.
- **Cover-the-name works on all three voices** without attribution: trade vocabulary and one-question-then-wait (Goliath), the restate-then-object structure (Paladin), fact-with-no-adjectives-to-nobody (Gaia).
- **The tonal drop is unsignalled.** The chapter does not brace before the apology notes. It changes subject the way a day does.
- **No numbers from anyone but the AI who owns them.** Gaia says *"a hundred metres"* as a sightline, which is her worldview; she never rates, tallies or counts. **Counting belongs to Spector alone.**
