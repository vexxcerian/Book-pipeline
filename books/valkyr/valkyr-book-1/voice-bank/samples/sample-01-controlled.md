# sample-01 — CONTROLLED
**Type:** controlled · **Source:** the author's own prose, Chapter One (`research/original-draft.md`)
**BENCHMARK #1 — the global voice at rest.**

---

He remembered walking out of that building for the last time in a uniform that already felt like it belonged to someone else, the base noise around him — dropship engines spooling, drill instructors barking somewhere out past the motor pool, the particular clatter of a mess hall at 1100 — receding behind him like a tide going out. He'd expected relief. He got something closer to vertigo. Twenty-six years of his life had a shape, and the shape had just been surgically removed, and what was left was an outline with nothing in it.

---

**Why this is the benchmark.** Everything the voice does, in three sentences.

- **Bimodal rhythm, exposed.** A 58-word accumulating sentence, then *"He'd expected relief."* (four words), then *"He got something closer to vertigo."* (six). No middle register anywhere.
- **The emotion lands at the very end of the long sentence**, after the clauses have done the work — never announced at the front.
- **Feeling displaced onto a physical fact.** Not "he felt unmoored." *An outline with nothing in it.*
- **The similes are physical, never explanatory.** *"like a tide going out"* describes a sound receding. It does not analyse his psychology.
- **Zero emotional temperature. Zero metacognition.** Neither appears anywhere in 14,776 words of draft, and neither may appear in the manuscript.
- Contains one instance of *"the particular ___"* — the author's most calcified construction. New chapters are rationed to **≤0.25 per 1,000 words**.
