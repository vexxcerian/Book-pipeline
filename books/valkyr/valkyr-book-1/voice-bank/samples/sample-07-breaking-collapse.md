# sample-07 — BREAKING (the collapse structure)
**Type:** breaking · **Source:** calibrated for the manuscript (Ch.13, memory-unlock stage 3). Not the author's prose.

---

Jameson came up on the open channel with a range and a bearing. Nothing else. A range and a bearing, in the voice he used for everything.

The interface went sideways.

Not off. Sideways. A half-second of readout that had a treeline in it, and the treeline was wrong, was not this treeline, had different light in it and a different hour of the day. Then it was gone. Then it was there again.

Vexx put his shoulder into the wall and did not fire.

Then a voice in his ear, and it was Rx's voice and it was not the voice Rx had now — flat, young, unprocessed, coming through nothing, coming through no speaker Vexx could name — saying three words and stopping. Saying the same three words. Stopping.

*The read is—*

Motor assist lagged. Two seconds. His right hand answered late and something went past his head close enough to move the air and he did not know afterward what it had been.

*The read is—*

Then nothing at all, for eleven seconds, and Vexx counted them the way you count a man's breathing.

Rx.

Nothing.

Rx.

Bandwidth, Rx said. A beat late, and then too fast. Bandwidth issue. I'm — it's the relay. We're fine.

---

**How the voice breaks here: the prose degrades with the interface.**

**Rules it demonstrates:**
- **Paragraphs shorten to nothing. The retrospective narrator disappears entirely.** No sentence in this passage knows more than the second it is in — which is the *opposite* of the book's default mode, and that reversal is the point.
- **One repetition, with a word different.** *"Saying three words and stopping. Saying the same three words. Stopping."* One. Not four. Restraint is what keeps this from becoming a stylistic showcase.
- **The tic breaks.** *"Bandwidth issue. I'm — it's the relay."* Rx reaches for the too-quick denial and cannot land it cleanly. **After Ch.13 it may never be smooth again.**
- **Minimisation by vocabulary, under load.** He downgrades a memory-seal rupture to *bandwidth*. That is Rx's cognitive distortion doing its job in real time.
- **NO Rx interiority.** Everything here is what Vexx hears, sees on a readout, or feels in a motor assist. Not one clause asserts what Rx experiences.
- **NO RAMPANCY.** No timer. No degradation rate. No "how long." His instability is memory suppression, trauma and moral weight, and nothing else, in any book of this series.
- **Stage 3 only.** He gets sound, image and a fragment of his own refusal. **No name, no face, no place.** Stage 4 belongs to Book Two.
