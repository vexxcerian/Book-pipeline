# sample-13 — BREAKING (the joke that stops)
**Type:** breaking · **Source:** calibrated for the manuscript (Ch.20). Not the author's prose.

---

Ashgrove, Rx said.

"Is that a place."

I don't know what it is. It's a word with a shape around it. There's a slope in it and the light's wrong for the hour and I can't tell you anything else about it, and I've been trying to tell you about it for eleven minutes.

Vexx waited.

You know what this is like, Rx said. This is like the time you swore blind you'd hit the eight-ring at four hundred and the spotter had the plate in his hand and the plate had

Nothing.

The cursor kept its rhythm. Vexx watched it do that for a while.

Sorry, Rx said. Sorry. That's — I don't know where that went. I'm going to need you to not ask me again tonight.

"Okay."

I'm not saying no. I'm saying not tonight.

"I know what you're saying."

---

**How the voice breaks here: the joke does not arrive.**

**Rules it demonstrates:**
- **This is Rx crying in a grocery store.** A mundane, undramatic, unearned break: the one thing he has always been able to do — land a joke — stops working mid-clause, and the silence after it is the worst sound in the book.
- **The marksmanship tally, deployed at the worst possible moment.** His irrelevant obsession, fourth of four appearances, and this is the one where it fails.
- **The apology is for the inconvenience.** *"Sorry. That's — I don't know where that went."* He apologises the way a piece of equipment would, which is the closest the book comes to saying what four years has done to him — and it never says it.
- **Stage-3 residue, not stage 4.** He produces a place-name he cannot attach to anything, a slope, and wrong light. **No face. No name. No rank. No date.** If in doubt, give less.
- **No Rx interiority.** *"The cursor kept its rhythm"* is Vexx watching an interface. Nothing here asserts what Rx experiences.
- **No rampancy.** Not one word about capacity, degradation or time remaining.
- **The chapter this belongs to contains no document interruption at all.** Ch.20, Ch.23 and Ch.26 belong wholly to people.
